# Weapon model ideas

These are concepts, not implemented replacements. Keep the existing weapon's
fire rate, damage, recoil, spread, reload timing, ADS behavior and collision.

| Weapon | Preferred visual | Alternatives | Fit and clearance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scorpion Evo | A scorpion with pincers around the muzzle | Staple gun; compact leaf blower | Tail curled along the side, below the sight line; pincers outside the center |
| Kar98k | Fishing rod with a reel beside the bolt | Broomstick; long baguette | Thin forward silhouette; reel can follow bolt animation without changing timing |
| Lynx | Actual M1A0 ginger cat | Original procedural cat as an optional fallback | Preserve the M1A0 tail; check hipfire and ADS in the real client |
| MP7 | Industrial nail gun | Cordless drill; toy water pistol | Compact body, existing magazine/reload clearance |
| P90 | Toaster with a long bread-slot magazine | Handheld vacuum | Keep the top low enough to see the front sight |
| Shotgun | Fire extinguisher | Plunger; party popper | Hose or plunger head stays away from the center; effects keep original origins |
| AWM | Long goose, beak forward | Telescope; long camera lens | Neck follows barrel axis; wings folded against the body |
| RPG | Leaf blower with a wide nozzle | Traffic cone launcher | Existing muzzle position and projectiles remain unchanged |

Best first pair: Scorpion -> scorpion, Kar98k -> fishing rod. Start with rigid
visual prototypes and inspect hipfire, ADS, reload, third person and LODs before
adding optional moving parts. No replacement is accepted from an armory preview
alone.
